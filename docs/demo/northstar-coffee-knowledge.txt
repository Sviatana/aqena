Company overview

Northstar Coffee is an independent specialty coffee company based in Portland, Oregon. The company operates two neighborhood cafés, an online store, a coffee subscription program, local catering, and a small wholesale program for offices and hospitality businesses.

Northstar Coffee focuses on traceable specialty coffee, seasonal menus, simple food, and practical customer service. The company roasts coffee in small batches three times per week.

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time, excluding major US holidays.

ONLINE ORDER PROCESSING

Orders placed before 12:00 PM Pacific Time on a business day normally begin processing the same business day.

Orders placed after 12:00 PM normally begin processing the next business day.

Online orders are not processed on Saturdays, Sundays, Thanksgiving Day, or Christmas Day.

Most coffee orders leave the roastery within 1–2 business days.

During major promotions or the December holiday period, processing may take up to 3 business days.

An order confirmation email does not mean that the order has already shipped.


SHIPPING WITHIN THE UNITED STATES

Standard shipping:
Estimated 3–7 business days after dispatch

Expedited shipping:
Estimated 2–3 business days after dispatch

Shipping estimates are not guarantees.

Northstar Coffee currently ships online retail orders only to addresses within the United States.

Northstar Coffee does not currently ship retail online orders internationally.

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address.


FREE SHIPPING

Orders of $50 or more qualify for free standard shipping within the contiguous United States.

The $50 threshold is calculated after discounts and before tax.

Gift cards do not count toward the free-shipping threshold.

Expedited shipping is never free unless a specific promotion explicitly says otherwise.

Shipping to Alaska or Hawaii does not qualify for the standard free-shipping promotion.


ORDER CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

Customers should contact orders@northstarcoffee.example as soon as possible if they need to change or cancel an online order.

Northstar Coffee can usually change or cancel an order only before it enters packing.

Once an order has been packed or handed to the carrier, it cannot be cancelled.

Changing a shipping address is not guaranteed after an order has been submitted.

If a package has already shipped to an incorrect address supplied by the customer, Northstar Coffee will help the customer review available carrier options but does not guarantee replacement or refund.


RETURNS POLICY

Customers can return unopened coffee products within 30 days of purchase with a receipt.

This 30-day rule applies to unopened retail coffee purchased in a Northstar café or from the Northstar Coffee online store.

Opened coffee is normally not returnable because coffee is a consumable food product.

If coffee is defective, damaged, stale on arrival, or clearly incorrect because of a Northstar Coffee fulfilment error, the customer should contact support even if the package has been opened.

Unused merchandise such as mugs or unopened brewing equipment may be returned within 30 days if it is in resalable condition and the customer has proof of purchase.

Used brewing equipment is not eligible for a standard return unless it is defective.

Original shipping charges are not refundable unless Northstar Coffee made an error or the product arrived damaged.


DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ORDERS

Customers should report damaged, missing, or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery.

The customer should provide:
- Order number
- Short description of the problem
- Photo of the shipping box if visibly damaged
- Photo of the incorrect or damaged item when practical

After review, Northstar Coffee may offer:
- Replacement
- Store credit
- Refund to the original payment method

Northstar Coffee does not require a customer to return a damaged bag of coffee unless support specifically requests it.


REFUND TIMING

Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method.

Northstar Coffee normally processes an approved refund within 2 business days.

Banks and card issuers may take an additional 3–10 business days to display the refund.

Northstar Coffee cannot speed up a bank’s internal posting time after a refund has been submitted.


LOST OR DELAYED PACKAGES

A carrier delay does not automatically mean a package is lost.

Customers should wait until the carrier’s estimated delivery window has passed before requesting a lost-package review.

If tracking has shown no movement for 5 consecutive business days, customers may contact orders@northstarcoffee.example.

Northstar Coffee will review the shipment and, where appropriate, open a carrier enquiry.

Replacement or refund decisions are made after reviewing tracking information and the delivery circumstances.


LOCAL PICKUP

Pickup orders are normally ready within 2 business hours during café operating hours.

Customers receive a “Ready for pickup” email when the order is available.

FRESHNESS AND STORAGE

Whole-bean coffee is best used within approximately 4 weeks of the roast date for peak flavour.

Coffee remains safe beyond that period when stored correctly, but aroma and flavour may gradually decline.

Recommended storage:
- Keep coffee sealed
- Store at room temperature
- Keep away from direct sunlight
- Keep away from moisture
- Avoid storing an opened bag in the refrigerator

For longer storage, customers may freeze tightly sealed portions and allow each portion to return to room temperature before opening.


ROAST DATES

Retail coffee bags show a roast date rather than an expiration date.

Online coffee is normally shipped within 7 days of roasting.

Northstar Coffee does not guarantee that every bag shipped on the same day has the same roast date.

Seasonal demand and production schedules can create small differences between batches.


BREWING GUIDANCE

Basic pour-over starting recipe:
Coffee: 20 g
Water: 320 g
Water temperature: 200°F
Total brew time: approximately 3 minutes

Basic French press starting recipe:
Coffee: 30 g
Water: 500 g
Water temperature: 200°F
Steep time: 4 minutes

Basic home drip starting ratio:
Approximately 60 g of coffee per 1 litre of water

These recipes are starting points, not strict guarantees. Grind size, water chemistry, brewer design, and personal preference may require adjustment.

CUSTOMER ACCOUNT SECURITY

If a customer believes their account has been accessed without permission, support should advise the customer to:
1. Change the account password
2. Use a unique password not reused on another service
3. Review recent orders and subscription changes
4. Contact support if any unrecognized transaction or address change is visible

Support should not disclose another customer’s order, address, phone number, or account information.


COMPLAINT HANDLING

Staff should acknowledge the customer’s issue without inventing facts or making promises that are outside policy.

If the answer is not available in company knowledge, support should say that the information needs to be confirmed.

Appropriate example:
“I don’t have enough confirmed information to answer that accurately. Please contact Northstar Coffee support so the team can check it for you.”

Inappropriate example:
“I’m sure we can refund that immediately.”

Refunds, replacements, catering exceptions, and shipping exceptions depend on the specific order and should not be promised without the required review.

Question:
